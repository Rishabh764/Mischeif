Our story

NO RULES.
JUST FIT.

Mischeif started in 2019 as a two-person print run in a garage. Six years later we're still small-batch, still independent, and still making clothes for people who'd rather cause a scene than blend into one.

How it started

A garage, a screen press, and too much attitude

We printed our first hundred tees on a secondhand press and sold them out of a backpack at shows. No investors, no five-year plan — just a name that felt right and a habit of doing things our own way. That habit stuck.

Today every piece is still designed in-house, sampled obsessively, and produced in small runs so nothing sits around long enough to feel ordinary.

What we stand for

The Mischeif code

Three things we don't compromise on, no matter how big we get.

01

Built heavy, not disposable

We use heavier fabrics and garment-dye everything so it looks better with wear, not worse. Fast fashion this is not.

02

Small batches, on purpose

We'd rather sell out of a drop than warehouse dead stock. Less waste, more reason to actually cop it when it's live.

03

Designed to be worn hard

Every sample gets stress-tested by our own crew before it ships. If it can't survive us, it's not going out.
